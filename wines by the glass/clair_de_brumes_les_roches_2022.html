Producer

Clair de Brumes is a single-estate project within the Saget La Perrière family, based in Pouilly-sur-Loire. The Saget family have been growing Sauvignon Blanc here for nine generations, with roots dating back to 1790 when they planted their first vines in the hamlet of Le Bouchot. The estate is currently led by Jean-Louis, Arnaud, and Laurent Saget. In the 1920s they became one of the first local bottlers in the region, and from the 1980s Jean-Louis expanded the family's reach across the entire Loire Valley. Today they farm approximately 230 hectares across all four Loire sub-regions, but Pouilly-Fumé remains their spiritual home. Clair de Brumes was launched with the 2022 vintage as a way to showcase individual terroirs within the appellation — prior to this, the estate's production was blended into broader cuvées.

Viticulture

Les Roches is a tiny 1.01-hectare single plot situated on the banks of the Loire in the commune of Pouilly-sur-Loire. The vineyard grows on rocky limestone terrain and faces southwest, making it an early-ripening site that consistently produces some of the estate's most concentrated fruit. The name "Les Roches" (The Rocks) references the stony, mineral-rich soil that is the defining characteristic of this parcel. All viticulture is certified organic, reflecting the family's commitment to sustainable, eco-friendly practices.

Winemaking

The wine is fermented by vineyard of origin to preserve the specific character of the plot. Following fermentation, it is aged for 12 months on its fine lees with regular bâtonnage (stirring), which builds texture, weight, and complexity — an unusually long lees contact for Sauvignon Blanc from this region. This extended ageing gives the wine a richer, creamier mouthfeel than the more typical crisp, lean Loire style.

Tasting Notes

On the nose, the wine is intense and complex, revealing aromas of citron, blackcurrant bud, quince, candied yellow fruits, and mirabelle plum. On the palate it is soft and elegant, with intense flavours of white peach and a distinct minerality leading to a round, ample, and long finish. The style is dense and radiant with genuine ageing potential — more structured and generous than a typical Pouilly-Fumé.

Talking Points for Guests

The Saget family have been making wine on this land since 1790 — nine generations of unbroken history in Pouilly-sur-Loire. At just one hectare, this is a genuinely small-production single-vineyard wine; it is about expressing one precise piece of land rather than blending across the appellation. The 12-month lees ageing is unusual for Sauvignon Blanc from the Loire and gives the wine a richer, creamier character than guests might expect. The name "Clair de Brumes" means "light from the mists" — a poetic reference to the morning fog that settles over the Loire riverbanks at dawn, enveloping the sleeping vines and shaping the microclimate of this unique terroir.

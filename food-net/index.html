Our Producers

Know who grows and catches our ingredients — these are the stories guests want to hear.

Keltic Seafare
Dingwall, North of Inverness — Est. 1992

Founded by Alasdair Hughson, Keltic sources exclusively from the North-West Highland coast of Scotland. They supply lobster, live langoustine and hand-dived scallops — each order hand-packed at their Dingwall factory and delivered within 24 to 36 hours of harvesting, ensuring everything arrives alive and fresh.

Used for: Isle of Skye Scallops
Meadowland Smokery
Colyton, Devon — Est. 2017

A family business founded by fish farmer Neil Fuzzard and his son Ben, a chef. They raise fish in enclosures and smoke wild eel following traditional methods. Meadowland supplies over 50 Michelin-starred restaurants across the UK and is prized for a product made with genuine craft rather than industrial scale.

Used for: Smoked Eel
Sladesdown Farm
Dartmoor National Park, South Devon

Owned by Daniel and Elaine, both raised on family farms. Originally raising turkey and geese for the holiday season, they have expanded to produce high-quality duck, chicken and pork. All poultry is reared on non-GM, all-natural feed and grazed on wild grasses, vetches and brassicas — giving the birds rich flavour, natural tenderness and exceptional juiciness.

Used for: Chicken Supreme, Duck & Asparagus Tarte
Flying Fish Seafoods
Brixham, Newlyn & Plymouth markets

Flying Fish uses dedicated buyers sourcing from the finest British fish markets — Brixham, Newlyn and Plymouth — plus special seafood from Scotland and the Rungis market in Paris. They also support local fishmongers who deliver directly to the door. After years perfecting their logistics, they deliver to restaurants within 48 hours of the catch.

Used for: Fish and seafood dishes
